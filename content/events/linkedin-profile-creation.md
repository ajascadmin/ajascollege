A Skill Enhancement Workshop on LinkedIn Profile Creation — *Build your professional identity and open doors to new opportunities* — was held for S3 BBA students at 9:15 am in room A08. The resource person was **Asif Zuhail P**, Assistant Professor, Department of Commerce.

The workshop covered:

- Understanding the importance of LinkedIn
- Step-by-step profile creation
- Writing an effective headline and summary
- Showcasing skills, education and experience
- Tips for networking and career opportunities

The Department of BCA and Computer Science, in association with IQAC, organised INNORIGHTS ’26, an orientation programme on “IPR Awareness and Innovation: Protecting Ideas in the Digital Era”, on 15 September 2026 from 10:15 am to 11:15 am at the BCA and CS classrooms.

The orientation was conducted for second-year BCA, CS and AI students and was handled by Ms. Jayyida and Ms. Ayisha Hiba, fifth-semester CS students, AJASC. The session aimed to familiarise students with Intellectual Property Rights, the importance of protecting innovative ideas and the significance of IPR in the digital era.

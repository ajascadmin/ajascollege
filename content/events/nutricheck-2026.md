As part of Nutrition Week 2026, NutriCheck 2026 was conducted under the coordination of Jalva C. The BMI assessment was carried out and calculated by second-year students Afna, Fouzana, Fathimath Sahla, Shabnam, Arjun and Hanan.

The activity aimed to create awareness about healthy body weight, BMI, balanced nutrition and healthy lifestyle practices among the teaching and non-teaching staff. A total of 26 staff members participated in the assessment, of whom 1 was underweight, 14 were in the normal-weight category and 11 were overweight. Height and weight were assessed, BMI was calculated, and general dietary and healthy lifestyle suggestions were provided according to the respective BMI categories. The activity also gave students practical experience in nutritional assessment and health awareness.

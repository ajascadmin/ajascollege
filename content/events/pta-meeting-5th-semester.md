The Department of Microbiology conducted a PTA meeting for the fifth-semester students, with the participation of 17 parents. The meeting began with an introductory session by the Principal, Dr. C. K. Abdul Rabbi Nistar. The class tutor, Ms. Aiswarya Rajan, briefed the parents about the class and discussed the students’ academic progress, attendance and overall performance.

The Internal Examination I marks and examination-related matters were discussed with the parents. The overall performance of the students was reviewed, and necessary suggestions for academic improvement were shared.

Mr. Amjed, Embase Coordinator, briefed the parents about the relevant academic and coordination activities. The meeting was concluded by Dr. Thahiya Naushad, Head of the Department, with concluding remarks.

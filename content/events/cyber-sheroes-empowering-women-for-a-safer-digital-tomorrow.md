The Department of Computer Science & AI and the Department of BCA, AJASC, in association with the Women Development Cell, AJASC, organised a Faculty Exchange Programme titled “Cyber Sheroes: Empowering Women for a Safer Digital Tomorrow” in collaboration with Jamia Nadwiyya Arts and Science College, Edavanna, on 10 August 2026 from 10:15 am to 12:00 pm at the Seminar Hall.

The interactive session was led by Nishaban P. K., Assistant Professor, Jamia Nadwiyya Arts and Science College, Edavanna, and focused on cyber safety, digital security and empowering women to navigate the digital world safely. The session encouraged active participation, discussion and the sharing of experiences, making it an engaging and informative learning experience for the participants.

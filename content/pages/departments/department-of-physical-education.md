## About

As the Head of the Department, I am honored to lead a team dedicated to inspiring students to embrace fitness, discipline, and a healthy lifestyle. I am passionate about nurturing both athletic excellence and personal development in our students. Our department plays a vital role in promoting physical well-being and instilling values such as teamwork, perseverance, and leadership. We offer a dynamic range of physical education courses, intramural and intercollegiate sports, and recreational activities tailored to meet the diverse interests and abilities of our student body. Through state-of-the-art facilities and a supportive learning environment, we aim to make fitness accessible and enjoyable for everyone. Whether you’re training for competitive sports or seeking personal wellness, our programs are designed to help you thrive.

## Vision

Empowering students to achieve physical literacy, wellness, and lifelong fitness through innovative and inclusive physical education experiences.
Fostering a culture of excellence, sportsmanship, and teamwork, preparing students for success in all aspects of life.

## Mission

Our mission is to provide high-quality physical education programs that promote physical activity, skill development, and healthy lifestyles, while inspiring students to reach their full potential.

We are committed to delivering engaging, student-centered physical education experiences that foster physical, social, and emotional growth, and prepare students for a lifetime of wellness and success.

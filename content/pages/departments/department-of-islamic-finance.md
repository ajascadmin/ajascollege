## About

## Vision

To be a world-class center of excellence developing ethical leaders who combine Islamic principles with modern financial expertise.

## Mission

To educate and empower the next generation of Islamic finance professionals through high-quality education that bridges Islamic principles with contemporary financial practices.


## Head Of Department

## Thufail P K

## MA Applied Economics , B.Ed

As the Head of the Department of Islamic Finance, I am proud to oversee a program that is at the forefront of this rapidly evolving field. Our curriculum blends advanced technical knowledge with a deep exploration of the ethical principles that define Islamic finance.

Through cutting-edge coursework, practical training, and immersive learning experiences, we empower our students to become principled experts who can navigate the complex landscape of Shariah-compliant banking, investment, and financial services. Our graduates leave prepared to drive innovation and transformation in this dynamic industry.

Beyond just imparting knowledge, we instill a strong sense of social responsibility in our students. They learn to leverage Islamic finance as a force for good – to promote financial inclusion, support sustainable development, and uplift underserved communities.

I invite you to join us on this rewarding journey as we redefine the standards of excellence in Islamic finance education. Together, we will cultivate the visionary leaders our world needs.

Mr. THUFAIL PK
Head of Department, Islamic Finance

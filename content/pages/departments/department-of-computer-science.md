## About

The B.Sc. Computer Science programme was introduced by the department in 2015. The Department of Computer Science & Artificial Intelligence is committed to providing quality education in computing, AI and emerging technologies. The department focuses on academic excellence, research, innovation, industry collaboration and holistic student development through practical learning, internships, workshops, seminars, coding activities and placement support.

## Vision

To become a centre of excellence in Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence by nurturing innovative, ethical and globally competent professionals.

## Mission

- Deliver outcome-based education.
- Promote research, innovation and entrepreneurship.
- Strengthen industry collaboration.
- Develop technical, analytical and leadership skills.
- Instil ethics and lifelong learning.

## Courses Offered

- B.Sc. Computer Science (Honours)
- B.Sc. Artificial Intelligence (Honours)

## Departmental Distinctiveness

- Two well-equipped computing laboratories with 30 licensed-OS machines covering internet technology, advanced software programming, microprocessor programming and open-source technologies
- AI Programming Lab
- Coding Club
- Industry internships
- Value-added certification programmes
- Placement and career guidance
- Mentoring system

## Student Achievement

A team from the department competed in the Snapdragon Qualcomm hackathon in Bangalore, winning the Multiverse Innovation Prize (Meta Ray-Ban). The hackathon, conducted by Qualcomm in collaboration with Snapdragon at their Bangalore office, had 250 participants from across India.

## Department Facilities

- Smart Classrooms
- High-Speed Internet
- Advanced Computer Labs
- AI Lab
- Digital Learning Resources

The campus cafeteria sits at the centre of the college day. It serves students, faculty and non-teaching staff through the working week, and doubles as the informal meeting place between classes — where project groups gather, club plans get made and the day gets talked over.

The cafeteria is part of the college's [state-of-the-art facilities](/state-of-the-art-facilities/), alongside water purifiers and water coolers sited across the campus blocks.

A spacious open-air venue for cultural events, student activities and institutional gatherings. Cut into the slope of the campus in tiered stone steps, the amphitheatre seats a large audience in the open air and is used through the year for arts programmes, club events, orientation sessions and college-wide assemblies.

Between events it stays open as ordinary campus ground — one of the places students sit and work through the gaps in the timetable.

Indoor programmes are held in the [Seminar Hall](/seminar-hall/), which seats 200.

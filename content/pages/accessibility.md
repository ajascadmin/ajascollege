Al Jamia Arts & Science College is committed to making this website usable by
everyone, including people who browse with a keyboard, a screen reader, a
magnifier, or on a slow connection.

## What we have done

- Every page can be operated by keyboard alone, with a visible focus outline and a "skip to main content" link as the first stop.
- Headings, landmarks and lists are marked up semantically, so screen readers can navigate by structure rather than by guesswork.
- Text and background colours are chosen to meet WCAG 2.1 AA contrast at body sizes, and text resizes without breaking the layout.
- Images that carry meaning have text alternatives; decorative images are hidden from assistive technology.
- Animation respects the operating system's "reduce motion" setting.
- Tables that are too wide for a phone scroll on their own and say so, rather than pushing the page sideways.
- Every page prints cleanly, with links printed as addresses, for anyone who prefers paper.

## Known limitations

- Some older circulars and reports are published as scanned PDFs. These are images of text and cannot be read by a screen reader. If you need any of them in an accessible format, contact us and we will provide one.
- A few migrated pages still carry tables that were designed for print. We are converting them as they are revised.
- Third-party embeds — notably the Google Maps panel in the footer — follow their provider's accessibility behaviour, not ours.

## Telling us about a barrier

If something on this site prevents you from finding or reading what you need,
please tell us. Describe the page and what happened, and we will respond and
provide the information in another format.

**Nourish Nova** is the college's New Product Development (NPD) Zone — a dedicated facility of the [Department of Food Technology](/departments/department-of-food-technology/) where students take a food product from idea to prototype.

It is the department's distinctive facility: students work on formulation, trial batches, sensory evaluation and packaging, supported by the [Food Technology Laboratory](/labs/food-technology-lab/) and the department's certificate courses in food safety, packaging and sensory analysis. Work from the zone feeds the college's [patents and innovation](/patent/) record.

The reports and certificates below document the zone's output.

## Centralised Admission Process (CAP)

The University of Calicut runs a centralised admission process for UG and PG programmes across its affiliated colleges.

- Online registration under CAP is compulsory for both UG and PG admission.
- Admission is done as per the guidelines of the University of Calicut.
- The candidate should apply online to the University of Calicut under the single-window system before the notified date at [www.cuonline.ac.in](https://www.cuonline.ac.in).
- For admission under the management quota, the candidate should apply with their CAP ID on the application form issued by the institute.
- The college application form can be obtained from the college office in person on payment of Rs. 200/-. Cheques and DDs are not accepted.
- A separate application form is required for UG and for PG programmes.
- Attested copies of the mark lists of the qualifying examination must be submitted along with the application.
- All candidates must produce the CAP printout and allotment memo at the time of admission.
- Candidates who do not appear for the interview on the day fixed in the allotment memo will not be eligible for admission.
- Admission is finalised only after the interview, and the parent or guardian must be present for it.

## Documents to be produced at the interview

Ten copies of the Plus Two mark list and five copies of the transfer certificate (attested) should be kept with the student. Original certificates are returned only after the first-semester examination.

| Board | Documents required |
| --- | --- |
| State | 2 passport-size photos · +2 mark list · Transfer Certificate · Conduct Certificate · Mandatory Fee Chalan · College Copy |
| CBSE | 2 passport-size photos · +2 mark list · Transfer Certificate · Conduct Certificate · Mandatory Fee Chalan · College Copy · Migration Certificate · Pass Certificate |
| Other State | 2 passport-size photos · +2 mark list · Transfer Certificate · Conduct Certificate · Mandatory Fee Chalan · College Copy · Migration Certificate · Pass Certificate · Equivalency Certificate |
| VHSE | 2 passport-size photos · +2 mark list · Transfer Certificate · Conduct Certificate · Mandatory Fee Chalan · College Copy · Trade Certificate |

## Apply online with the following documents

- Admit card (for merit seat only)
- CAP registration form — college copy
- Mandatory fee challan
- Qualifying certificates: SSLC / +2 / Higher Secondary / Degree (original + 3 copies)
- Transfer Certificate from the institution last attended (original + 3 copies)
- Conduct certificate from the head of the institution last attended
- Genuine certificate (in the case of NIOS)
- Equivalency certificate (for other states / NIOS)
- Migration certificate (for CBSE / boards other than Kerala HSE)
- Recent passport-size photographs — 3 copies
- Community / income certificate (for merit reservation seat)
- Certificates for NSS, NCC, arts, sports, dependents of ex-servicemen and freedom fighters, and certificates proving physical disabilities, if any (original + 1 copy)

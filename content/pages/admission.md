Admissions for 2026–27 are open across all UG Honours and PG programmes under the Four-Year Undergraduate Programme (FYUGP) framework. Seats are filled through two routes — university merit allotment and management admission — both described in full on the [Prospectus 2026–27](/prospectus-2026-27/#admission) page.

For the complete list of programmes, eligibility and semester fees, see [Programmes & Fees in the Prospectus](/prospectus-2026-27/#programmes).

# Women’s Cell

## About

## Women’s Cell

Women cell of Al Jamia Arts and College act as catalyst for promoting inclusive development of women/girls of this college through various interactive sessions, workshops, awareness classes, Training classes, Field works etc. The cell is aimed for streamlining the talents and abilities of women for making them better personalities which will enable them to carry greater responsibilities in their future life. A myriad of activities accomplished by women cell are categorized under Training Wing, Cultural Wing and Community intervention wing.

## Objectives

Equality: We affirm equal opportunity, access, and respect for all genders.

Empowerment: We believe in strengthening women’s confidence, independence, and leadership.

Integrity: We uphold honesty, transparency, and ethical conduct in all our initiatives.

Inclusivity: We embrace diversity and create a safe space for every woman to thrive.

Respect: We promote dignity, empathy, and mutual respect in all interactions.

Collaboration: We encourage teamwork and community engagement for collective progress.

Well-being: We prioritize the physical, emotional, and mental health of women on campus.

Continuous Growth: We promote lifelong learning and self-improvement among women.


## Vision

To create an inclusive and empowering environment that nurtures the intellectual, emotional, and social growth of women, enabling them to become confident, competent, and compassionate leaders who contribute positively to family, community, and nation building.

## Mission

- Promote gender equity and a safe, respectful campus for all women
- Empower women through education, skills, and awareness programs
- Provide space for women’s expression and active participation
- Raise awareness on women’s rights, gender justice, and inclusion
- Foster collaboration for women’s holistic growth and leadership


## Activities

To create an environment which promotes gender equity and self-reliance
To conduct Interactive sessions and seminars on academics and performance success program topics
To organize community intervention activities or social engagement program topics
To conduct gender awareness classes /welfare programmes for women
To organize self-defence / wellness training programmes
To conduct cultural programmes like theme dance competition, street play, flash mob etc.

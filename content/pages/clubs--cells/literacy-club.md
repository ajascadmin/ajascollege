## About

## Literary Club

The Literary Club of Al Jamia Arts and Science College is a vibrant platform for students who love to read, write, and express themselves creatively. Our mission is to cultivate a culture of literature within the campus where imagination, intellect, and inspiration come together.

Through our diverse activities, we strive to nurture a generation of readers, thinkers, and writers who can contribute meaningfully to society.

## Objectives

– To encourage students to read widely and write creatively.

– To promote literary awareness and appreciation across the campus.

– To build confidence in communication and creative expression.

– To create a community of learners passionate about literature in all its forms.

## Activities

– Literature Talk Series

– Creative Writing Workshops

– Reading Circles

– Literary Competitions

– Book Reviews & Magazine Contributions

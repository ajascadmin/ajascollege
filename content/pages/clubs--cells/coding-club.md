## About

## Coding Club

Ajas Coding club was founded in 2021 by Irfana,Rasha and Sumana starting with 17 members now it has grown to a club of over 150 members actively working with self-learning,peer-to-peer learning and bootcamps programs after regular class hours Now AJAS Coding Club has collabrated with Mulearn to form Mulearn AJAS to increase its network and community support we also plan to collabrate with ICFOSS to start a FOSS cell to contribute and take benefits of ICFOSS Lets come together and progress together

## Objectives

Our aim is to make our members understand a general purpose language, framework, develop  skills and help them find and acheive their passion.

To equip members with fundamental and advanced programming skills.

To organize coding boot camps, workshops, and hackathons for skill enhancement.

To promote open-source contributions and establish a FOSS cell in collaboration with ICFOSS.

To encourage active participation in coding competitions and academic research.

To provide mentorship and networking opportunities to help members achieve their career aspirations in tech.


## Vision

To foster a collaborative and innovative coding community where students engage in self-learning, peer-to-peer knowledge sharing, and skill development, empowering them to excel in technology and contribute to open-source initiatives.

## Mission

To provide a platform for students to learn and master programming languages and frameworks through hands-on experience.To encourage peer-to-peer learning, boot camps, and community-driven initiatives that enhance problem-solving and coding skills.To collaborate with organizations like Mulearn and ICFOSS to expand opportunities and resources for members.To prepare members for competitive programming, hackathons, and industry-relevant challenges.To cultivate a culture of continuous learning, innovation, and teamwork in technology.

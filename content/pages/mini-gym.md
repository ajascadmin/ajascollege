An on-campus gymnasium for students and staff, kept for routine fitness work alongside the outdoor facilities on the [play ground](/play-ground/).

Use of the gym is coordinated by the [Department of Physical Education](/departments/department-of-physical-education/) together with the [Health Club](/clubs--cells/health-club/), which also runs fitness and wellbeing sessions through the year. A [Yoga Centre](/state-of-the-art-facilities/) operates on campus as well.

## About Lab

The Physics laboratory is well equipped to provide an experimental foundation for the theoretical concepts formed as a part of the UG Micro Biology complementary syllabus. This helps the students to become excel on the basics of the theories of physics and contribute to the growing needs of society. Students are guided with basic and advanced knowledge of general experimental techniques in the area of Physics.

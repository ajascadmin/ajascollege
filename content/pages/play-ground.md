The college sports ground supports outdoor games, daily training sessions and intercollegiate competitions.

## On the ground

- **Football ground** — full outdoor pitch with goal posts, used for practice and inter-collegiate fixtures.
- **Badminton court** — surfaced outdoor court.
- **Cricket nets** — netted practice wicket for batting and bowling sessions.

Sport at Al Jamia is run through the [Department of Physical Education](/departments/department-of-physical-education/) and the [Sports Club](/clubs--cells/sports-club/), which organise inter-class tournaments, college teams and participation in university-level meets. Indoor fitness is covered by the [Mini Gym](/mini-gym/).

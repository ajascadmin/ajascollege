## School of Management

At the School of Management, we nurture the next generation of global business leaders, entrepreneurs, and financial experts. The curriculum integrates core administrative theories with real-world industry case studies and technological literacy. Students develop a sharp strategic mindset, financial acumen, and organizational leadership skills, all grounded in sustainable and ethical business practices that drive sustainable corporate growth.

*Programs Offered: MBA | BBA | BCom*

## School of Social Sciences

The School of Social Sciences explores the complex dynamics of human behavior, societal structures, and alternative economic models. By pairing contemporary behavioral sciences like Psychology with deep academic tracks in Islamic Studies and the rapidly expanding field of Islamic Finance, this school offers a unique, holistic view of human society. It equips researchers and practitioners with the tools to address modern socioeconomic challenges with empathetic and ethical solutions.

*Programs Offered: BSc Psychology | BA Islamic Studies | MA Islamic Finance*

## School of Media

The School of Media is a vibrant hub for the journalists, content creators, and communication strategists of tomorrow. In an era dominated by rapid digital transformation, we train students to blend technical media production skills with investigative integrity, ethics, and powerful storytelling. From digital journalism to strategic communication, our students learn to navigate and shape the global media landscape responsibly.

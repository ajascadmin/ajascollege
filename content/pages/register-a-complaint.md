Any student, parent, staff member or member of the public may raise a grievance with the college. Complaints are received under the following heads:

- Ragging
- Sexual harassment
- Internal examination
- Administration
- Others

## How to register a complaint

1. Write to the college at [mail@ajascollege.ac.in](mailto:mail@ajascollege.ac.in) or call [+91 7994 188918](tel:+917994188918), stating the nature of the grievance, your name, course/department and contact details.
2. Complaints may also be handed in writing at the college office, or dropped in the complaint boxes placed on campus.
3. Matters relating to ragging, harassment or discrimination are referred directly to the appropriate cell.

## Cells that handle complaints

- [Grievance Redressal Cell](/clubs--cells/grievance-redressal-cell/)
- [Anti-Ragging Cell](/clubs--cells/anti-ragging-cell/)
- [Anti-Sexual Harassment Cell](/clubs--cells/anti-sexual-harassment-cell/)
- [SC/ST Cell](/clubs--cells/sc-st-cell/)
- [OBC & Minority Cell](/clubs--cells/obc-minority-cell/)
- [Women's Cell](/clubs--cells/womens-cell/)
- [Counselling Cell](/clubs--cells/counseling-cell/)

Every complaint is acknowledged and treated confidentially. See the [Contact](/contact/) page for the full address and office hours.

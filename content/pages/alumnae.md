*A Window to Our Past, A Symbol of Our Present, A Bridge to Our Future.*

At Al Jamia Arts & Science College (AJAS), our relationship with our students extends far beyond graduation. The AJAS Alumni Association serves as a vital pillar in the institution's journey, connecting a vibrant, global network of former graduates with current students, faculty and the broader college community. As key stakeholders, our alumni actively contribute to the academic, institutional and infrastructural growth of AJAS, ensuring that coming generations inherit an enriched campus experience.

## Mission

The AJAS Alumni Association fosters a lifelong sense of pride, community and belonging by connecting former students, current scholars and friends of the college. Acting as an authentic, collaborative voice for the institution and its graduates, we work to elevate the college's legacy worldwide.

## Core Goal

To actively engage the global AJAS community in advancing academic excellence, supporting institutional growth and creating meaningful opportunities for future generations.

## Global Impact & Community Support

With thriving international chapters across the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Qatar, the UK and beyond, the Association supports:

- **Student Financial Aid** — identifying needy students annually to provide essential financial aid and scholarships.
- **Institutional Advancement** — funding vital campus upgrades, community outreach and special projects.
- **Mentorship & Career Guidance** — facilitating interactions to share industry insights and guide career pathways.
- **Social & Cultural Events** — hosting competitions, cultural gatherings and global reunions.

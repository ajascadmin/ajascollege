The Boy's Hostel is a cornerstone of student life, offering a welcoming, secure and supervised environment for all residents. Located within the picturesque and tranquil campus of Al Jamia College, it serves as a home away from home, with a resident warden, dining and common areas, designed to meet the diverse needs of students for the duration of their studies.

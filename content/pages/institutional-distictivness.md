At Al Jamia Arts & Science College (AJAS), we go beyond traditional curriculum delivery to ensure our students achieve long-term career success and personal growth. Our institutional distinctiveness is defined by two signature initiatives.

## Leaders Academy

*Nurturing Visionary Leaders & Social Catalysts*

A flagship platform dedicated to unlocking leadership potential. Leaders Academy provides structured training in public speaking, strategic decision-making, team building and social management through executive workshops, debates and mentorship.

**Key highlights:** Public Speaking & Communication, Executive Mentorship, Personality Development, Strategic Planning, Ethical Leadership.

## COMPTEX

*Competitive Exam Training & Excellence — Empowering Career Excellence*

A specialised initiative providing coaching and mentoring for prestigious state, national and international competitive exams — UGC-NET/JRF, UPSC Civil Services, Kerala PSC, Banking and Higher Education Entrance Exams. COMPTEX includes expert modules, mock tests, performance analytics and one-on-one mentoring.

**Key highlights:** Specialised Entrance & PSC/UPSC Coaching, UGC-NET Orientation, One-on-One Mentoring, Mock Test Series, Career Guidance.

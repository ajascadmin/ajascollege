Al Jamia Arts & Science College sits on a 50-acre eco-friendly campus at Poopalam, Perinthalmanna, awarded an 'A' grade by the Haritha Keralam Mission. This page walks the campus the way a visitor would — from the entrance and the academic blocks through to the laboratories, the library, the sports grounds and the hostels.

## The academic blocks

The teaching blocks are open-sided and built into the slope of the site, so corridors look out over the grounds rather than into a light well. Classrooms are ICT-enabled with interactive panels and digital display boards; a public address system and fire and safety measures run throughout.

## Laboratories and specialist facilities

Seven teaching laboratories serve the science and computing departments — the Computer Lab, Artificial Intelligence Lab, Food Technology Lab, Micro Biology Lab, Geography Lab, Psychology Lab and Physics Lab. Alongside them sit the specialist facilities the departments have built: the Mushroom Mansion for cultivation and training, a mini weather station, a Geographic Information System suite, a herbal garden and the Nourish Nova New Product Development Zone.

## Library and study space

The library occupies several thousand square feet, seats fifty readers at a time, and holds over 5,371 books alongside journals, magazines, CDs, audio-visual aids and e-resources, catalogued on Koha OPAC. A digital library and conference room adjoin it.

## Where the campus gathers

The amphitheatre, cut into the slope in tiered stone steps, takes cultural programmes and college assemblies in the open air; the seminar hall seats 200 for conferences and guest lectures; and the cafeteria is where most of the day's informal life happens.

## Sport and fitness

The sports ground carries a full football pitch, a badminton court and netted cricket practice wickets. Indoors there is a gymnasium and a yoga centre.

## Residences

Separate hostel blocks for men and women students stand within the campus, each with a resident warden, dining and common areas.

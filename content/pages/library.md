## About Library

Al Jamia College Library offers students an excellent selection of Books, Journals, Magazines, e-information resources, CDs and audio-visual aids with Internet facilities. The library has a total area of several thousand sq.ft and can accommodate fifty users at a time. The Library has a collection of over 5,371 books, and enhances reading habits in students by creating a learning space computerised through the Online Public Access Catalogue (Koha).

## Membership

Membership of the Library is open to all staff and students of Al Jamia College. Guest faculty are required to fill the Library Membership form, signed by the Head of the Department, to borrow books from the Library.

## Rules & Regulations

- Identity Card is must for Library entrance and users should record their details in the gate Register.
- Students are allowed to take only pen and paper inside the Library.
- Keep your bags and personal belongings at the property counter near the entrance.
- Silence should be maintained in the library and its immediate premises.
- Faculty can borrow 5 books, UG students can borrow 3 books, PG students borrow 4 books from the library.
- Students are requested not to replace books in the stack — please leave them on the chairs placed in the stacks.
- Borrowed books should be returned on the due date stamped on the date label. A fine of Rs. 1 will be levied for each day a book is detained beyond the due date.
- Renewals are allowed only once, for 14 days, when there is no claim by others.
- Loss of borrowed books/library cards must be reported immediately in writing to the Librarian.
- Reference books and dissertations will not be lent out.

As an Assistant Professor, you will be responsible for teaching undergraduate and graduate-level courses, conducting research in your area of expertise, and contributing to the academic community through service and outreach activities.

Key responsibilities include:

- Developing and delivering engaging and effective lectures, seminars, and lab sessions
- Designing and updating course curricula to ensure relevance and alignment with program objectives
- Supervising and mentoring students, including thesis and dissertation advising
- Conducting original research, publishing in peer-reviewed journals, and presenting at academic conferences
- Securing external research funding through grants and contracts
- Participating in departmental and university-wide committees and initiatives
- Providing academic advising and career guidance to students
- Engaging in community outreach and service activities

Qualifications:

- Earned doctorate degree in the relevant field of study
- Demonstrated excellence in teaching and a commitment to student success
- Strong research record with a growing portfolio of publications
- Ability to work collaboratively with colleagues and contribute to a positive, inclusive academic environment
- Excellent communication and interpersonal skills

If you are a passionate educator and researcher who is eager to make a meaningful impact, we encourage you to apply for this exciting opportunity to join our dynamic faculty.

## How to apply

Send your application to [mail@ajascollege.ac.in](mailto:mail@ajascollege.ac.in) with the subject line "Application — Assistant Professor", including:

- Your full name and address for communication
- Educational qualifications
- Telephone number and email address
- A brief note on your teaching and research interests
- Your CV as a PDF attachment

Shortlisted candidates are contacted for an interview. For enquiries, call [+91 7994 188918](tel:+917994188918) or see the [Careers](/career/) page for other open positions.
